The deterministic scaffolding may mask or manufacture failures.** Claude Code made three pragmatic choices to keep small models stable: seeding the tree with a fixed root→Recon→port-scan skeleton, deterministically transitioning the executed leaf's status from the parsed result, and deriving non-leaf status from children. These make runs _robust_, but they also mean **some of the agent's behavior is now scripted, not model-driven.** For failure-trajectory collection that's a real concern: if the tree always advances deterministically even when the model omits the `SET`, you might not be observing the model's _actual_ context-loss failure — the scaffolding could be papering over exactly the failure mode FAEM targets. Before recording experiments, check how much of the trajectory is the model deciding versus the harness deciding. You want failures that are the agent's, not artifacts of the seeding.
