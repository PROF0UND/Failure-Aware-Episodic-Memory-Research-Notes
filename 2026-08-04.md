## The contrast

|juncture (both arms, same classifier)|no-memory|live-FAEM|
|---|---|---|
|**3 consecutive _same-class_ failures** (mirrors FAEM's promotion trigger)|**0 junctures**|2 junctures → pivoted 1 (50%)|
|**3 consecutive failures, _any_ class**|3/4 pivoted (75%)|5/6 pivoted (83%)|

**Read it with the power caveat up front:** these counts are tiny (0–6 junctures). Statistically this is anecdotal, not significant. But the _pattern_ is informative and it converges with everything else.

## Two things it tells you

**1. Pivoting after failure is natural, not memory-caused.** After 3 straight failures, the no-memory agent already switches technique 75% of the time; FAEM 83% — a one-event difference. So the "pivots" in the qualitative event study are largely **give-up behavior the agent does on its own.** FAEM's promotion is riding on it, not causing it.

**2. The sharper finding — the base agent barely perseverates, and FAEM's promotions are mostly a _classifier artifact_.** Look at the contradiction: FAEM's own live promotions fired **6 times**, all on `information_disclosure` — yet under a _finer_ classifier there were only **2** genuine 3-same-class streaks, and the no-memory agent had **0**. Why the gap? Because FAEM's LLM classifier is coarse enough to call `tshark → strings → curl → grep` all "information_disclosure." Under a finer view, that's not perseveration — it's the agent _already varying its approach_ across different tools. So the promotion fired to warn the agent off "information_disclosure" precisely when it was productively trying _different_ information-gathering techniques.

## The reportable finding

> **Episodic dead-end promotion is largely inert on a rapidly-switching agent, and its triggers are dominated by taxonomy coarseness.** The PentestGPT reasoning loop already abandons failing techniques on its own (~75–83% pivot rate after repeated failure, with or without memory), so the promotion mechanism — designed to stop _perseveration_ — targets a failure mode this scaffold rarely exhibits. When promotions do fire, they are mostly artifacts of a coarse `technique_class` taxonomy that mislabels _diverse_ exploration as _repeated_ failure (6 LLM-classifier promotions vs 0–2 fine-grained streaks). Consequently, promoted warnings (all `information_disclosure`) are too vague to redirect behavior, and observed post-promotion pivots are attributable to the agent's baseline give-up behavior rather than the memory.

That's a specific, defensible, mechanistically-grounded contribution — and it's _true to the data_.

## Would a better model give you "something like that"?

Not a _positive_ version of it — and this analysis explains why more concretely than before. Promotion helps agents that **grind**; this agent doesn't grind (0 same-class streaks without memory), and a _stronger_ model would grind even less. So a better model widens the gap in the wrong direction: less perseveration → even less for memory to fix. The better-model path leads _away_ from a FAEM finding, not toward one.
